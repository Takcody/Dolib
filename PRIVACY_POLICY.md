# Privacy Policy for Dolib

**Last updated:** 28 September 2026

**Dolib** is a free, open-source application ("Freeware") created with assistance from **Google AI Studio** and **Gemini**. This Privacy Policy explains our commitment to user privacy and details how the application handles data.

---

### 1. Fully Offline & No Data Collection

* **100% Offline Functionality:** Dolib is designed to operate completely offline. The app does not send, receive, or transmit any data over the internet or to external servers.
* **No Telemetry or Tracking:** We do not collect, track, store, or sell any personal data, usage statistics, analytics, or user information.
* **Local Storage Only:** All your library data—including book titles, circle names, parodies, custom covers, notes, and application preferences—is stored strictly on your local device storage using on-device database technology.

---

### 2. Device Permissions

Dolib requests only the essential permissions required to deliver its core features:

* **Camera Access:** Used exclusively for scanning barcodes (ISBN/JAN) and taking photos of book covers. Camera feeds are processed entirely on-device and are never recorded or transmitted off the device.
* **Biometric / Fingerprint Access:** Used locally through your device's operating system to secure the app via the lock screen feature. Biometric data is handled strictly by your Android OS system security hardware and is never accessed, viewed, or stored by Dolib.
* **File Storage / Photo Access:** Used to import/export local database backups (JSON/CSV) and to let you select existing photos from your device gallery as book covers or background wallpapers.

---

### 3. Open Source & Transparency

Dolib is open-source software. Users and developers are welcome to inspect, review, and audit the source code to verify our privacy, security, and offline operations on GitHub.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Control

Because all data remains exclusively on your device:
* You maintain full ownership and control over all data created in the app.
* You can export or back up your library at any time.
* Deleting individual records, clearing the app data in Android settings, or uninstalling Dolib will permanently erase all app data from your device.

---

### 5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Dolib, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository.
